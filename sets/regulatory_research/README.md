# Regulatory research

A domain of [techieray training lab](../../README.md).

Legal and compliance teams depend on knowing what the law actually says, where it is published and whether it
applies. The training sets in this domain teach AI systems the research skills behind that work, using real
developments recorded in the Global AI Regulation Tracker.

## Training sets

| Set | Skill it trains | Tasks | Read more |
|---|---|---|---|
| Find primary source | A research agent finds the official document behind news of an AI law or policy development, or recognises that none exists | 50, including 5 with no official source | [find_primary_source](find_primary_source/README.md) |

More sets in this domain will follow as they are released.

## Using the sets

Each set's data and documents are in its folder here, and its code is in
`src/techieray_training_lab/regulatory_research/<set>/`. Each set's README explains its tasks, how outputs are scored
and its rules.
